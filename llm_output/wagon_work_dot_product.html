Pulling a wagon: the dot product is the useful work

Drag the hand (the end of the rope) to change how hard and at what angle you pull. Change how far the wagon rolls with the slider. Then hit Pull! to roll it.

A — your pull (along the rope) forward part (this does the work) vertical part (wasted, just lifts) B — the wagon's motion

What to try

1. Flatten the rope (drag the hand down near the ground) → almost all your pull becomes useful, work is near maximum. cosθ → 1.
2. Pull straight up (drag the hand directly above the handle) → the forward part collapses to 0, the wagon does not roll forward at all. Work = 0. This is exactly what "perpendicular → dot product 0" means — every bit of effort is wasted on lifting.
3. Pull backward (drag the hand to the left of the handle) → the forward part goes negative, you are braking. Work < 0.

The dot product silently deletes the wasted vertical part every time — that is the whole point of cosθ. It keeps only the effort that lines up with where the wagon actually goes.
